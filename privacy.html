TwinBoy privacy policy

Effective October 18, 2026.

TwinBoy does not collect personal data, track you, show ads or use analytics. No account is required. The app does not send your games, saves or settings to a server.

Games you import are copied into TwinBoy's local storage. Save states, cartridge saves and preferences stay on your device. Your operating system may include this data in device backups according to your settings. If you choose a file from a cloud provider in Files, that provider handles the download under its own privacy policy.

You can remove imported games from the game library. Their saves remain so you can import the same file again. Deleting the app removes its local data. Copies in device backups are managed through your device's backup settings.

Apple may process App Store, TestFlight and diagnostic information under Apple's privacy policy and your sharing settings. TwinBoy contains no third-party tracking or analytics services.
