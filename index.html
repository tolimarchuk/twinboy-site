TwinBoy

A pocket handheld for your games

TwinBoy turns your phone into a handheld for 8-bit and color handheld games you own. Made for foldables: unfold it and hold it upright, and the screen sits on top with a real controller below the fold. Folded, it is a classic handheld on the outer screen.

  • Load your own .gb and .gbc files from Files
  • Battery saves, three save-state slots, fast forward
  • Six shells, six screen palettes, pixel-perfect screen
  • Game controllers and keyboards supported
  • Includes Tobu Tobu Girl by Tangram Games

No account, no ads, no tracking. TwinBoy does not include or download commercial games.
